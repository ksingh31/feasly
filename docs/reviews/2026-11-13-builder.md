# Builder portal: design review and redesign plan

Scope: `/builder/*` (login, org picker, leads dashboard, lead notes and status, billing and card, invoices, team, record a signed contract). Inputs: 92 screenshots in `audit/shots/builder/`, `audit/report.json`, source under `apps/web/src/app/features/builder/`, `docs/design-tokens.md`. Read-only review; nothing in the repo was changed.

## Executive summary

1. The portal works, but it is laid out like an internal admin tool. Every page is a stack of white cards with labelled fields and `Apply` buttons. Nothing tells a builder "here are your 2 hot new leads, call them."
2. The core loop (new lead → contact → quote → won → record contract → invoice → paid) is spread across 4 nav items. The dashboard shows it as 9 identical tall cards with no sorting, and phone and email can't be tapped.
3. Money is the weakest area. The same invoice is described three ways (banner "overdue", table "In review", copy "7 days later" vs "7 days after the review window"). Billing has no plan, balance or "what you owe" summary. Recording a $18,750 commission has no review step.
4. Mobile at 390px is broken for 2 of the 6 admin pages. The Invoices and Team tables scroll sideways, which hides amount, status, role and every action.
5. Visually: there are 194 hex literals across builder SCSS, an off-palette gold banner, 4 container widths, 3 heading sizes and system-font buttons on login. It doesn't feel like the warm, premium consumer site.

**Redesign direction:** a phone-first "lead inbox" portal. A **Today** home with prioritised new and stale leads plus one money strip. A **Lead detail drawer** that holds contact (tap to call/email), a status stepper, notes, and "Record contract" inline. A single **Money** area (balance, upcoming auto-charges with countdown, invoices, card, plan). Everything is built from one shared portal UI kit with the admin console (data table that becomes cards on mobile, drawer, modal, confirm, chip, page header, empty state, money strip), using only design tokens.

## Design findings

### Must-fix

1. **There's no "what do I do now" on the home screen.** The dashboard leads with up to 3 full-width gold invoice bars (about 250px; on mobile they fill the first screen, `dashboard__mobile__loaded__due-invoice-expanded-viewport.png`), then 9 equal cards in creation order.
   - Sort new leads by score and age.
   - Show "New: needs first contact" and "No update in 7+ days" groups at the top.
   - Collapse all invoice alerts into one compact money strip ("$54,150 due · 1 failed card · next auto-charge tonight").
2. **Leads can't be acted on from a phone.**
   - Make phone and email tappable (`tel:`, `mailto:`).
   - Add a "Call", "Email" and "Directions" action row on every lead.
   - Status change takes three steps (select, then Apply, then nothing visible happens). Replace it with a one-tap status stepper or segmented control, plus an undo toast. Ask for confirmation only for Won (routes to Record contract) and Lost (asks for a reason).
3. **Lead cards are too heavy and repeat the same chrome.** Each card is about 130 to 450px tall and repeats six uppercase field labels. The notes column takes 35% of the width even when it's empty, and repeats a gold "Add the first note" CTA 4 times on one screen. Use a compact row or card: name, chip, score, timeline, last activity and a quick-action icon. Move full details and notes into the lead drawer. Notes expanding inline (`dashboard__desktop__loaded__lead-notes-open.png`) leaves a large blank left column.
4. **Responsive tables (B1–B3).** One `DataTable` component that renders as a stacked card list under 768px. Show the primary identifier and amount on the first line, the status chip on the right, and the secondary line underneath. No horizontal scroll ever.
5. **Money clarity.**
   - **Use one status model across banner, table, detail and lead card:** Draft → In review (auto-charges in N days) → Charging → Paid / Failed / Disputed (paused) / Manual (cheque, e-transfer due).
   - **Use one charge-timing sentence** everywhere, e.g. "We charge your card 7 days after the invoice is issued. You can question it any time before then."
   - **Billing page needs:**
     - Plan: flat plan or 1% commission, with the agreed rate. Today the rate only appears in Record contract copy, and it says 1.5%.
     - Outstanding balance, next charge date and card.
     - Billing email.
     - A "Question this invoice" contact link (mailto or form). Disputes are deferred in code (`builder-invoices.component.ts:69`), but copy says "contact Feasly" with no link anywhere.
6. **Recording a contract creates a charge without a review step.** Add a summary confirm before submit: "Create invoice for $18,750 (1.5% of $1,250,000). Your Visa •••• 4242 will be charged on Nov 19 unless you contact us." After submit, show a success state with the invoice and countdown. Today members can't record wins at all because the route is admin-only, so give members a "Mark won, ask admin to record" path. Otherwise wins go unreported, which is revenue risk.
7. **Destructive confirms are unsafe and generic** (`team__desktop__loaded__deactivate-confirm-viewport.png`).
   - No title, and a generic "Yes, continue" in brand gold.
   - The dialog is pinned 32px from the top instead of centred.
   - Use the shared `ConfirmDialog`: title ("Deactivate Marco Silva?"), consequence body, danger-styled verb button ("Deactivate"), Cancel focused by default, Esc to close.
8. **Brand tokens and typography (B11).**
   - Add a global form-control font reset.
   - Replace the 194 hex literals in builder SCSS with tokens: due-banner `#dcb43a` becomes a new documented `--warning` / `--warning-bg` pair; failed uses `--danger`.
   - Use one page header style: Instrument Sans 600 at a single product-H1 size. Today it's 32px on Leads and Team, 24px on Billing, Invoices and Record contract.

### Should-fix

1. **Container widths are inconsistent:** Leads and Team use a 1104px tinted panel inside the cream page, Invoices 1024px, Billing 896px, Record contract 704px. Some pages are wrapped in a `--cream-2` "card in a card" and others aren't. On mobile that wrapper adds a second gutter (about 40px a side). Use one `PageLayout` with a single max-width of 1120px and a narrow variant for forms, and drop the tinted wrapper.
2. **Navigation:** 5 text links + email + Sign out in a flat row. "Record signed contract" is a nav item, but it's an action and belongs as a primary button and inside the lead drawer. The email shows as plain grey text. Move it to an account menu (avatar → org switcher, email, Sign out). On mobile, use a bottom tab bar (Leads, Money, Team) instead of a full-screen hamburger sheet.
3. **Status chips:** small pastel chips in five unrelated colours (blue New, green Contacted and Won, gold Quoted, purple Disputed). Contacted and Won are both green. Define one `StatusChip` with a token-mapped semantic palette (neutral, info, progress, success, warning, danger, paused), used by leads, invoices and team.
4. **Invoice detail renders inline**, replacing the list ("← Back to invoices"), with label/value rows pushed to opposite edges of a 1000px card (hard to scan). Use a right `Drawer` on desktop and a full-height sheet on mobile.
   - **Header:** amount, chip and countdown.
   - **Body:**
     - Breakdown: contract value × rate = commission.
     - Payment method.
     - Timeline in chronological order.
     - Actions: Download PDF/receipt, Question this invoice, Change payment method.
   - The method select with a pale disabled "Apply" floats with its label misaligned (`invoice-detail-panel-viewport.png`). Make it a single "Change" link that opens a small modal.
5. **Pending-edit pattern everywhere** (select + Apply on lead status, team role, billing default method, invoice method). Disabled Apply buttons are pale gold and low contrast. Make the change immediately with an undo toast, or use an explicit modal for money-affecting changes such as the payment method.
6. **Team:**
   - Add a "You" tag on the current user.
   - Pending invites need "Resend" and "Revoke" actions (not Deactivate + Remove).
   - Deactivated users need a greyed row and no role select.
   - Explain "View as" (tooltip: "See the portal as Marco sees it").
   - In the invite modal: the disabled "Send invite" button looks like a muted brand button, and there's no role explanation ("Members see leads; Admins also see billing and team"). On mobile it should be a bottom sheet with a sticky action bar.
7. **Empty states are bare text boxes** with no illustration and no next step.
   - **Invoices:** "No invoices yet". Add "Record a signed contract" and "How billing works" links.
   - **Team (only yourself):** add "Invite your sales lead" guidance.
   - **Record contract with no leads:** a dead end with no link.
   - Use a shared `EmptyState` component (icon, title, one line, primary action).
8. **Loading and error:** skeletons exist on Leads and Billing; check Invoices and Team have them too. Error panels are inconsistent (some role=alert text, some with Retry, org picker without). Use one `ErrorState` with Retry.
9. **Login:** a plain white card in a sea of cream. No wordmark logo, no product context, no help link. Add the brand mark, a single line of value ("Your Feasly leads, quotes and invoices"), and "Trouble signing in? Contact us".

### Polish

- Avatars are initials on brass-tinted circles (fine); add status-colour rings for New.
- Format project type ("New build", "Renovation"), timeline ("0–3 months"), relative dates ("2 days ago", with absolute on hover).
- Lead score: show as a 0–100 heat pill (Hot ≥85, Warm 65–84, Cool <65) instead of a bare "92".
- Notes preview: keep the "Feasly team" badge in the collapsed preview so builders notice Feasly guidance.
- Due banner meta: "(change it on the invoice)" should be a link.
- Use tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in money columns, and right-align amounts.
- Record contract: the order should be lead → value → date, then the commission panel last as a summary. Drop the "What happens next" card that repeats the page subtitle.
- Mobile record contract: long lead option text truncates ("…Calgary, A"). Use the drawer flow instead (record from the lead).
- Focus ring is fine (brass); make sure the danger button has a danger-tinted ring.

## Proposed redesign

### IA and navigation

```
Leads (home)          ← default; inbox + pipeline toggle
  └ Lead drawer       ← contact, status stepper, notes, timeline, "Record contract"
Money                 ← admins only
  ├ Overview          ← balance, next auto-charge, card, plan
  ├ Invoices          ← DataTable → Invoice drawer
  └ Payment & plan    ← card (Stripe), default method, agreed rate, billing email
Team                  ← admins only
Account menu (avatar) ← org switcher (shows current org), email, sign out
```

- Desktop: left rail or a slim top bar with 3 items plus the account menu. Mobile: bottom tab bar (Leads, Money, Team) with the account sheet behind the avatar.
- "Record contract" stops being a nav item. It's launched from a won lead, and from a secondary button on Money → Invoices ("Record a contract") for leads not in the list.
- Members see Leads only, plus "Mark won", which notifies admins to record.

### Leads home concept: prioritised inbox with an optional pipeline view

- **Money strip** (admins, only when something needs attention): one line, colour by worst severity. Example: "1 card payment failed · $19,800 auto-charges tonight · $24,750 pending → View". It replaces the 3 gold bars. Failed is danger, due today is warning.
- **Needs you** section (always first):
  - New leads not yet contacted, sorted by score, with age ("2h ago").
  - Leads with no status change in 7+ days.
  - Won leads with no recorded contract ("Record contract to close out").
- **Pipeline** below:
  - Desktop: segmented filter (All · New · Contacted · Quoted · Won · Lost) with counts, replacing both the 6 total tiles and the redundant status dropdown. Plus a list/kanban toggle. Kanban has 4 active columns (New, Contacted, Quoted, Won); drag to change status, with Won/Lost asking for confirmation.
  - Mobile: list only, with horizontally scrollable filter chips.
- **Lead row:** avatar · name · heat pill · status chip · "Timeline 0–3 mo · New build · 3415 19 St SW" · last activity · quick actions (call, email, more).
- **Lead drawer** (right, 480px on desktop; full-screen sheet on mobile):
  - **Header:** name, chip, heat, Call / Email / Directions buttons.
  - **Status stepper:** New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost.
  - **Details:**
    - Estimate summary. Link to the homeowner's Feasly estimate if that's allowed.
    - Address, timeline, project type.
  - **Notes thread** with a composer pinned to the bottom.
  - **Activity timeline.**
  - **Won panel:** contract value, date, live commission preview, then a review confirm before creating the invoice.

### Billing clarity

- **Money overview card:**
  - "Outstanding: $54,150"
  - "Next charge: $19,800 tonight to Visa •••• 4242"
  - "1 payment failed: update card"
  - Plan: "Commission plan · 1.5% of signed contract value (excl. land)" or "Flat plan · $X/mo".
- **Invoice lifecycle** shown as a 4-step mini-stepper on every invoice: Issued → Review window (7 days, countdown) → Charged → Paid. Disputed shows "Paused: Feasly is reviewing".
- **Explain every number:** contract value × rate = commission, with land excluded and a tooltip.
- **Always offer "Question this invoice"** during the review window (contact link until disputes ship). Show the deadline: "Question by Nov 19 to pause the charge."
- **Card management:** the Stripe Element sits in a modal/sheet from "Update card", not appended at the bottom of the page (today `formOpen` renders the form below the default-method panel, far from the card panel it edits).
  - Style the Element with tokens: Stripe `appearance` with Instrument Sans, `--border`, `--accent` focus.
  - Show "Your card isn't charged now" reassurance and the Stripe security badge.
  - When there's no publishable key, show "Card updates are temporarily unavailable" only on the button's disabled state, not as a page-level contradiction.
- **Failed card:** a persistent danger strip in the shell (all pages) until resolved, with one CTA, "Update card", that opens the card modal directly.

### Mobile-first patterns (390px)

- Bottom tab bar; sticky page header with the page title and one primary action.
- Lists, never tables. `DataTable` switches to cards below 768px.
- Drawers become full-height sheets with a drag handle. Modals and confirms become bottom sheets with full-width stacked buttons (primary on top, Cancel below), safe-area padding, and a sticky action bar.
- Tap targets ≥44px. Call/email as large icon buttons. `inputmode="numeric"` and live currency formatting on contract value.
- Money strip collapses to one line ("2 invoices need attention ›").
- Single gutter of 16px; remove the nested tinted wrapper.

Build once in `apps/web/src/app/shared/ui/` (tokens only, OnPush, signal inputs) and use it in both builder and admin:
