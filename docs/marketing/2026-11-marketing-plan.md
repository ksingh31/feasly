# Feasly marketing plan — November 2026

**Status:** proposal for owner review · **Written:** 2026-11-14 · **Scope:** Calgary launch (Elite Craft Builders pilot), builder-side go-to-market, and expansion to Edmonton → Vancouver → Toronto.

> **How to read the numbers.** Sources are listed at the end, each with its date and the date accessed (all accessed 2026-11-14). Most published CPC and conversion benchmarks are US, in USD, and not specific to an industry. Converted figures use a planning rate of **1.38 CAD per USD**; check the live rate before setting budgets. No public source gives **Calgary-specific search volumes**, so volumes marked *est.* are planning estimates. Task #1 in week 1 is to replace them with Google Keyword Planner and Microsoft Ads Keyword Planner pulls for the Calgary location target. All dollar figures in this document are marketing and funnel numbers. Estimate figures shown to homeowners still come only from `packages/cost-engine`.

## 0. Executive summary

1. **Paid search works only if the funnel converts.** At 1% of a typical ~$750k construction contract (land excluded), Feasly earns **~$7,500 per signed build**. After leakage and card fees, the realistic breakeven is **~$6,200 of acquisition cost per signed contract**. On base-case assumptions, paid search costs **~$3,600 per signed contract**. That is profitable, but short of a 3:1 target (~$2,000). On conservative assumptions it costs ~$14,000, a loss. The Elite pilot exists to measure four conversion rates. Budget should scale only when they clear the gates in §5.
2. **Start paid search at Tier B (~$1,500/mo CAD).** This matches the ~$1.5k/mo test already planned in Epic 04. Use exact- and phrase-match "cost to build / infill / rebuild" intent only, plus ~10% on Microsoft Ads. Expect about 230 clicks, 45 estimates, 14 leads and 4 qualified leads a month.
3. **The biggest free win is SEO coverage.** All 40 live community pages were picked by assessment record count. The result is suburban communities (Panorama Hills, Saddle Ridge, Cranston…) plus Beltline and Downtown Commercial Core. **None of the core infill communities has a page:** no Altadore, South Calgary/Marda Loop, Killarney-Glengarry, Mount Pleasant, Capitol Hill, Renfrew, Parkdale or West Hillhurst. Adding ~25 established-area pages should come before most paid spend.
4. **Calgary's zoning changed under us.** Council repealed citywide (blanket) rezoning on 2026-04-08, effective **2026-08-04**. Most residential lots reverted to their pre-2024 districts. "What can I build on my lot?" is now the most confusing question for homeowners, and the best content and PR hook Feasly has. Every lot-potential claim in ads and content must reflect post-August zoning.
5. **Builder go-to-market:** founder-led sales to ~20 Calgary infill builders. Lead with the Elite pilot numbers, "no win, no fee" (1% of construction contract, land excluded), and leads that arrive already holding a range. Target: 2–3 more builders signed by the end of the 90-day plan.
6. **Expansion order holds, with one caveat.** Edmonton is easy: open Socrata assessment data with zoning and lot size, and lower CPCs. Vancouver works for the City proper: open property-tax data, a multiplex/laneway pitch, and higher CPCs. **Toronto is blocked by data.** MPAC assessment data is restricted and licensed, not open. Do not commit to Toronto until a data-licensing decision is made.

### 1.3 Account structure

- **Campaigns:** (1) Build cost (A); (2) Rebuild (B); (3) Lot potential (C); (4) Communities (E, one ad group per cluster of 3–5 communities); (5) Builder intent (A/D "builder" terms, Tier C only). Search network only, with no Display expansion and no search partners at launch.
- **Geo:** Calgary city, "presence" only. Not "presence or interest", which leaks out-of-province traffic.
- **Bidding:** Max Clicks with a CPC cap (~$9) until there are ≥30 `estimate_complete` conversions a month. Then switch to Maximize Conversions on `estimate_complete`. After ≥30 `lead_submitted` a month, move to tCPA on leads. Tier A never gets there, so it stays on manual/capped bidding.
- **Landing pages:** the estimator itself, deep-linked with the community pre-selected where possible, or the matching community page. Never the homepage.
- **Conversions:** `estimate_started`, `estimate_complete` (primary at launch), `lead_submitted` (CASL consent given), plus an offline import of `qualified` and `won` from the builder pipeline. The `/r/{token}` report pages must never fire ad pixels or enter remarketing audiences.
- **Microsoft Ads:** import the Google campaigns and give them ~10% of the search budget. Bing holds 15.9% of Canadian desktop search (StatCounter, Aug 2026), and this audience researches build costs on desktop. Microsoft CPCs are often lower, but confirm that in the account rather than assuming it.

**Ad-copy guardrails** (CLAUDE.md copy rules plus `docs/legal/approved-copy.md`):

- Never write "accurate", "exact", "guarantee(d)", "appraisal" or "market value". Never write "Unlock" in ads either; it is reserved for one use in the app.
- Say "planning range", "based on City-assessed data", "not a quote".
- Example: **Headline** "Calgary Infill Cost Range" · "See Land + Build + Total" · "Free, No Email to Preview". **Description** "Enter your Calgary address. See City-assessed lot data and a build-cost planning range in minutes. Not a quote."

## 2. Keyword strategy summary

Paid search should buy only *cost* intent: queries about building, rebuilding or the cost of a lot. "Builder" terms are expensive. Test them only once qualified-lead economics are proven. Everything about zoning, assessment or "what can I build" goes to SEO first, because the volume is large, CPCs are low and AI answers will lean on it (§3). Section 1.2 has the full grouped list.

### 3.3 AI-search visibility (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude)

Context:

- AI Overviews cut outbound organic clicks by **39.8%** in a randomized 1,000-user field experiment (SSRN, April 2026).
- Position-1 CTR on keywords with AI Overviews fell from **7.3% (March 2024) to 1.6% (December 2025)** (Ahrefs). Seer saw a partial recovery to 2.4% by February 2026.
- Local-service queries trigger AI Overviews least, because the local pack wins.

So Feasly needs to be **the cited source**, not just the ranked link. Actions:

1. **Keep llms.txt as the canonical fact sheet.** Add the established-area communities, a one-paragraph "what Feasly is / is not" (planning ranges, not quotes or appraisals), the cost-data version, and the date of the last data refresh.
2. **Allow the AI crawlers** in `robots.txt`: OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Bingbot. Copilot and ChatGPT search lean on Bing's index, so register with **Bing Webmaster Tools** and enable **IndexNow** pings on build.
3. **Structure answers the way engines extract them.** Put a 40–60-word direct answer at the top of each guide, then a table of ranges, then methodology. Add FAQPage JSON-LD and `Dataset` JSON-LD on the Cost Index.
4. **Earn third-party mentions.** Pitch the zoning guide and Cost Index to local media and community association newsletters. AI engines weight independent citations.
5. **Measure monthly.** Run a fixed panel of ~20 prompts ("how much does it cost to build an infill in Altadore", "what can I build on my lot in Calgary after the rezoning repeal", …) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini/AI Overviews, Copilot and Claude. Log whether Feasly is cited, and tag AI referrers in analytics.

## 5. Funnel economics

### 5.1 Revenue per signed contract

The commission base is the **gross construction contract, land excluded** (Epic 05, decision 3; Epic 08, A5). The default rate is 1%, configurable per builder.

Contract-value anchors:

- Feasly's own community ranges for a 2,400 sq ft build are **~$525k–$703k standard, ~$699k–$939k premium** (the engine output in `llms.txt`).
- Local guides quote **$250–$300/sq ft standard**, and $225–$400/sq ft for infills depending on spec (Havre, July 2025; bestcalgaryhomes).
- New inner-city infills list at **~$1.05M–$1.8M including land** (Mount Pleasant $1.299M, Capitol Hill $1.175M, Killarney $1.799M; listings, Nov 2026).
- The platform default `avg_contract_default` is **$750,000** (BLD-003).

| Construction contract (excl. land) | 1% (default) | 1.5% | 2% |
|---|---|---|---|
| $600,000 (standard, smaller) | $6,000 | $9,000 | $12,000 |
| **$750,000 (platform default)** | **$7,500** | $11,250 | $15,000 |
| $1,000,000 (premium / luxury) | $10,000 | $15,000 | $20,000 |

### 5.2 Allowable CAC (per signed contract)

```
Gross revenue per win (1% × $750k)                         $7,500
− Attribution leakage / disputes / unreported wins (≈15%)  −$1,125   (assumption; permit cross-check reduces it)
− Card processing (≈3%)                                     −  $191   (assumption)
= Net revenue per win                                      ≈ $6,184   ← breakeven CAC
Target CAC at 3:1 (cash arrives 3–12 months after spend)   ≈ $2,060  → use $2,000
```

### 5.3 Back-solving target cost per stage

The base conversion rates are planning assumptions until the pilot measures them. Industry close rates on *qualified* leads run 20–35% for remodelers (JLC, Sandler), but **4–8% for cold web leads** across all leads (trade sources). Custom builds are longer and larger, so the plan uses 10% from qualified lead to signed contract.

| Stage | Base rate | Target cost (for $2,000 CAC) | Breakeven cost (for $6,184 CAC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signed contract | — | $2,000 | $6,184 |
| **Qualified lead** (owns lot or is under contract; established-area Calgary address; start ≤18 months; budget within range; consent to builder contact) | 10% → signed | **$200** | $618 |
| Lead (email + CASL consent) | 30% → qualified | $60 | $186 |
| **Completed estimate** | 30% → lead | **$18** | $56 |
| Click | 20% → estimate | **max CPC $3.60** | max CPC $11.10 |

**What the table says.** Base-case paid search (~$6.50 CPC) is profitable but runs at ~1.7:1, not 3:1. Reaching 3:1 takes one or more of:

- **Estimate→lead from 30% to 50%** (better report value, follow-up email): cost per signed contract falls from $3,611 to ~$2,170.
- **Qualified→signed from 10% to 15%** (fast builder response; Elite's process): ~$2,410.
- **Contract mix from $750k to $1M**, or a builder-specific rate above 1%: revenue rises 33% or more.
- **Organic and partner traffic**, with CAC near zero, carrying most of the volume and blending CAC down.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5.5 Decision gates for paid spend (measured over 6–8 weeks of Tier B)

| Metric | Continue at Tier B | Scale to Tier C | Pause and fix funnel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per completed estimate | ≤ $35 | ≤ $25 | > $50 |
| Cost per qualified lead | ≤ $400 | ≤ $250 (sustained 6 weeks) | > $600 (≈ breakeven) |
| Builder first contact on new leads | — | median ≤ 24 h | > 72 h (leads go cold, regardless of ad spend) |

Signed contracts lag 3–12 months, so **qualified leads are the leading indicator**. Confirm qualification with Elite weekly, and feed won/lost back through offline conversion import.

## 6. Builder-side go-to-market

### 6.1 Who to sign next

The target is ~20 Calgary **infill and custom builders** doing ~5–40 established-area builds a year. They are big enough to need a steady lead flow and too small to run their own estimator or ad team. Sources for the list:

- BILD Calgary Region membership
- City of Calgary building-permit open data, filtered to new single/semi-detached builds in established communities, grouped by applicant
- infill listings by builder name

Avoid suburban production builders: their product is the show home in a new community, not your lot.

### 6.2 Pitch

> "Homeowners use Feasly on your website to see City-assessed lot data and a land, build and total planning range for their own address. You get the lead with that context already in hand, so the first call starts at 'here's my lot and my budget' instead of 'how much per square foot?'. You pay nothing until a lead you got from Feasly signs a construction contract with you: 1% of the construction value, land excluded."

### 6.3 Pricing framing

- **Pay on conversion, no win no fee.** A card is on file and charged only after a reported win plus a 7-day review (Epic 05).
- **Compare it to what builders already pay.** A Google Ads lead in the home-improvement category costs ~US$50–70 before any qualification (Focus Digital 2025; Search Engine Land 2025). A builder needs dozens of those per signed build, with spend at risk every month. Feasly's fee is incurred only on a signed contract.
- **The flat plan exists for high-volume builders.** The breakeven nudge (`N = ceil(flat_price / (avg_contract × 1%))`) shows when flat becomes cheaper. The flat price is still an open owner decision (Epic 05 Q2); set it after 2–3 months of pilot data.
- **Configurable rate.** Keep 1% as the public default. Reserve a different rate for exceptional cases (exclusive community territory, co-marketing spend) and document why.

### 6.4 Proof points to collect from the Elite pilot (start on day 1)

1. Estimates and leads per month, split by channel.
2. Lead→first-meeting rate, and how it compares with Elite's other lead sources.
3. **Time saved on the first call.** Elite's own words, quotable with permission.
4. First signed contract: a case study with the address anonymized and the contract value shown only as a range.
5. Homeowner quotes on what the range helped them decide. Testimonials must not claim accuracy.

### 6.5 Sales motion

- **Weeks 1–4:** build the 20-builder list and a one-page pitch with live demo links.
- **Weeks 5–8:** founder-led coffees and demos, plus one BILD Calgary event.
- **Weeks 9–12:** sign 2–3 builders, at most one per community cluster at first. Light exclusivity is a sweetener and avoids lead-routing conflicts before Epic 08 marketplace routing ships.
